### Decision: 2026-03-04 11:25 PST - undated pages get their own pile

**In plain words, we decided to:** put every page with no readable date in an undated pile, and never give it a guessed date

**Decision:** A page with no readable date goes to the undated pile.

**Decided by:** the project owner, 2026-03-04 11:25 PST

**Exact words:**

> If it cannot read the date, it should say so. A page in the wrong year is worse than a page in a pile I have to look at.

**Where recorded:** Chat with the Claude session on 2026-03-04.

**Effect:** The undated pile becomes part of every sort, and the release-notes text on this issue lists it under Changed. The practice numbers on this issue count a page in the undated pile as not read.

**Next step:** Describe the undated pile in the README, owner Claude session
