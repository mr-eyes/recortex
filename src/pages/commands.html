Commands

The observer works without being asked. These five commands, and one agent, are for when you want something now. Type them in Claude Code.

Each one starts with /recortex:. A bare /record is not promised.

/recortex:record [what]

Records what just happened in this conversation, or what you name. A plan you approved becomes a task with steps. It shows what it would post, posts it, and tells you where it went.

{{say /recortex:record the decision on idempotency keys }}

/recortex:ask <question>

Answers from the record only. The answer starts with "From the record:", gives the address of each note it used, and says what the record does not hold. It posts nothing.

{{say /recortex:ask why did we drop the queue-based retry? }}

/recortex:todo [add <text> | done <n>]

With nothing after it, shows the to-do list with its checkmarks. add puts an item on the list in your words. done checks an item off, by its number in that list.

{{say /recortex:todo add Alert when a retry gives up }}

/recortex:status

Tells you, in this order: where the project stands in short, the open tasks and their next steps, what text is due, and what waits for your yes. Then it asks you about what waits, one multiple-choice question each.

{{say /recortex:status }}

/recortex:usage

Gives what the plugin used on this machine, in tokens and calls against each daily limit, and offers the page with its usage view. It shows no money: under a Claude subscription these count against your usage limits.

{{say /recortex:usage }}

@agent-recortex:ask

A read-only agent that answers a question about the project's history from the record, in its own context, on Haiku. It quotes each note with its address, does not guess, and posts nothing. It can run only the read commands of index.py query, and a hook stops anything else. Good for a long question. If the mention does not start it, /recortex:ask is the other way in.

{{say @agent-recortex:ask what did we try for retries, in order, and what stands now? }}

Things you can just say

No command is needed for these. Claude knows what they mean.

  • Set up the record for this project.Starts setup: a few questions, a summary, and nothing written before you confirm.
  • pause hereLeaves a note of where things stand for the next session.
  • show me the mind of this projectBuilds and opens the private page.
  • catch up the recordBuilds the record from the history a project already has.
  • off the recordRequests a hold on the session’s unpublished notes. Check that the hold is active before sharing what you want to keep off GitHub.
